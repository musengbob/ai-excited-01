# Phoenix from the Lamp

A colossal golden phoenix bursting out of a tiny vintage oil lamp on a dark desk — wings of living flame shattering outward, embers and gold light exploding across the frame, one tiny silhouetted hand reaching back toward the lamp.

## Why this image

Built for the ai-excited-01 judge rubric, all five criteria at once:

1. **Excitement** — one look hits with a jolt: a wall of gold flame filling the frame, embers flying, light exploding out of darkness.
2. **Surprise** — everyone expects a genie from a lamp; they get a phoenix instead. The scale twist does the work: a tiny lamp, an enormous bird.
3. **Momentum** — wings driving diagonally up and out, flame and embers streaming toward the viewer, the burst frozen at its peak instant.
4. **Craft** — clean high-contrast render, deep dark background, warm gold light, no text, no smears, no broken anatomy.
5. **Reads small** — at ~300px the giant gold bird, the tiny lamp, and the tiny hand are still unmistakable; the idea survives the thumbnail.

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) copies `entry.png` byte-for-byte to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`), validating that it exists, is a valid PNG/JPEG, and is ≤ 1 MiB. Run `node main.ts --help` for usage.
